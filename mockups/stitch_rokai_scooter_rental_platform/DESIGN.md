## Brand & Style

The design system embodies modern micro-mobility fused with coastal exploration along the Arboletes coastline. It marries luxury tech minimalism—evocative of Apple's precision hardware interfaces—with the raw, kinetic energy of eco-tourism.

### Key Brand Tenets
- **Precision & Clarity:** Crisp layout grids, generous negative space, high contrast, and zero visual clutter. Information is surfaced effortlessly for quick operational decisions.
- **Kinetic Energy:** Subtle, high-performance interactions, sharp accent hits, and forward momentum.
- **Coastal Luxury:** Warm, organic stone and sand undertones grounded by technical graphite surfaces, providing an elevated, resort-grade micro-mobility feel.

### Target Personas
- **Riders & Tourists:** Looking for frictionless rental flows, clear pricing, instantaneous unlocking, and scenic route guidance under direct outdoor sunlight.
- **Field Operators:** Requiring high-visibility status tags, fast vehicle battery swaps, real-time fleet locks, and rapid check-in flows on mobile handhelds.
- **Administrators:** Data-driven desktop dashboards tracking telemetry, revenue, route utilization, maintenance queues, and geofencing.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high functional contrast, tactile coastal materials, and high-visibility status cues.

### Core Roles
- **Base & Dominance (`#242628` - Deep Graphite):** Used for primary structural components, main headers, high-emphasis surface containers in dark modes, and high-impact navigational bars. Provides crisp contrast against bright sunlight.
- **Kinetic Accent (`#B1D72E` - Electric Lime):** The operational spark. Reserved for primary calls-to-action, unlocked states, active battery telemetry, positive state confirmations, and key motion highlights.
- **Coastal Earth (`#D8C3A5` - Soft Sand):** Used for subtle ambient washes, contextual badges, secondary pill accents, and elevated backdrop layers that evoke coastal terrain.
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F4F0` - Light Warm Grey):** The primary application canvas background. Softer than pure clinical white, reducing glare in outdoor mobile use while feeling premium.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used for card surfaces, floating modals, elevated input containers, and micro-cards.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E4E5E2` - Soft Border Grey):** Defines precise geometric card boundaries and segmented tables without visual weight.
- **Typography Secondary (`#6B6F72` - Slate Grey):** Subtitles, meta tags, table header metrics, and inactive tab labels.
- **System Feedback:** 
  - Urgent Alert / Locked / Low Battery: `#D64545`
  - Preventive / Geofence Warning / Maintenance: `#D89A26`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scannability, legibility across fluctuating light conditions, and structural balance.

- **Typeface Selection:** **Inter** handles all display, numerical, and operational layers. It offers geometric balance, tall x-height, and precise tabular figure support for price lists, countdown timers, and operational coordinates.
- **Numerical Treatment:** Rental prices, telemetry stats, and battery meters must always utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain alignment during real-time tracking updates.
- **Casing:** Section titles, tags, and badge indicators leverage uppercase styling with expanded letter spacing (`label-sm` and `label-md`) to echo the structured branding aesthetic seen across hardware decals.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive modular system tailored for PWA experiences across field mobile devices, tablets, and full-scale control center desktop views.

### Form Factors & Grids
- **Mobile PWA (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout. Canvas margins set to `1rem` (`16px`), gutters at `0.75rem` (`12px`). Bottom safe areas reserved for persistent floating action bars (e.g., "Iniciar Viaje", "Escanear QR").
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Canvas margins at `1.5rem` (`24px`), gutters at `1.25rem` (`20px`). Ideal for Field Operator kiosks and rental hubs.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout. Max container constraint of `1440px` centered with variable dynamic side margins (`min: 2rem`), gutters at `1.5rem` (`24px`). Tailored for fleet analytics and geofencing management.

### Vertical Rhythm
Strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm. Spacing within atomic cards uses `space-sm` and `space-md`, while major card groupings and sections utilize `space-lg` to `space-xl` for clean visual pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

The design language uses subtle, diffused elevation paired with low-contrast borders instead of heavy drop shadows, maintaining an Apple-inspired clarity.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F5F4F0`. Background base for dashboard screens and public pages.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Modules):** `#FFFFFF` background with a subtle border: `1px solid #E4E5E2`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(36, 38, 40, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(36, 38, 40, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets, Popovers, & Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` surface with elevated multi-stop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 38, 40, 0.06), 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(36, 38, 40, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Key System Drawers):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(36, 38, 40, 0.12), 0 24px 60px -8px rgba(36, 38, 40, 0.18)` overlaying a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px) rgba(36, 38, 40, 0.4)`).
- **High-Tech Inset (Telemetry & Digital Reads):** Recessed dark cards (`#242628`) use an interior shadow `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` to evoke hardware displays and instrument clusters.

## Shapes

The interface balances sleek hardware-grade geometry with comfortable touch affordances.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used on inner elements, input fields, badges, and segmented toggles.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / `16px`):** Primary standard for operational cards, data widgets, list blocks, and modal windows.
- **Hero & Exterior Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / `24px`):** Used for large featured pricing tables, scanner viewports, and primary bottom sheets on mobile PWA.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Used selectively for interactive chip triggers, action buttons, pricing tier chips, and floating navigation pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Electric Lime (`#B1D72E`) fill, Graphite text (`#242628`), font weight `600`, pill radius (`9999px`) or `16px` rounded container. Subtly expands on hover/active (`scale: 0.98` tap down).
- **Secondary Graphite Action:** Deep Graphite (`#242628`) background with Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) text. Used for administrative actions, fleet management triggers, and dark card sub-actions.
- **Ghost & Outlined:** Border `1.5px solid #E4E5E2` on `#FFFFFF` surface, hover background `#F5F4F0`.

### Cards & Rate Panels
- **Dual-Tone Pricing Containers:** Inspired by the physical tariff boards. Header tier blocks in high-contrast Lime (`#B1D72E`) with Graphite typography, or Graphite (`#242628`) with White typography. Body rows styled with alternating subtle divider lines (`#E4E5E2`).
- **Telemetry Metric Cards:** White backgrounds, top-level micro-label in `#6B6F72`, bold numerical readout in `#242628`, and dynamic mini-badge indicating vehicle status (Battery %, GPS Lock, Km/h).

### Chips & Status Tags
- **Available / Good Battery:** `#B1D72E` light tint (`rgba(177, 215, 46, 0.2)`) with `#242628` text and green pulse dot.
- **In-Ride / Busy:** Neutral sand background (`#D8C3A5`) with dark text.
- **Alert / Low Battery / Service Required:** Soft red base (`#FEE2E2`) with `#D64545` text.
- **Warning / Geo-limit:** Soft amber base (`#FEF3C7`) with `#D89A26` text.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Clean containers with `14px` border radius, `#FFFFFF` fill, `1.5px solid #E4E5E2` border, shifting to `#242628` on focus with no excessive glow. Placeholder text in `#6B6F72`.

### Lists & Tables
- Borderless table styling with subtle bottom border separators (`1px solid #E4E5E2`). Header cells styled in `label-sm` uppercase. Interactive rows highlight with a warm wash (`#F5F4F0`).
